The pie chart (Chart 6, panel a: MFF and NGEU envelopes contributing to climate objectives, by programme; shares of total envelope devoted to climate objectives) shows that the Recovery and Resilience Facility alone provides 42% of EU climate-related funding, and together with the common agricultural policy and regional policy almost four-fifths.

- **Recovery and Resilience Facility**: 42% (the centrepiece of NGEU; all other instruments are part of the MFF)
- **Common agricultural policy**: 22%
- **Regional policy**: 14%
- **Other**: 6% (all facilities contributing less than €10 billion to climate mainstreaming, e.g. InvestEU)
- **Horizon Europe**: 5%
- **Connecting Europe facility**: 4%
- **NDICI** (Neighbourhood, Development and International Cooperation Instrument – Global Europe): 4%
- **Just Transition Mechanism**: 3%

Sources: European Commission and ECB calculations.
